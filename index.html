♥ Our mission

We share what we love. We show how to protect it.

KWP explores amazing creatures, plants and places, introduces the people and organizations protecting fragile habitats, and shows how even kids can make a real difference.

Safe, curious, classroom-ready

KWP is built to be a place parents and teachers can trust.

  • Comments off by default for a calmer, kinder space for kids.
  • Short, accurate episodes that pair well with science lessons.
  • Real conservation stories that spark questions and action.
